Pickleball

A Simple Guide to the Sport

About Pickleball

What is Pickleball?

Pickleball is a paddle sport played on a small court. It combines characteristics of tennis, badminton, and table tennis.

Equipment

Players use a paddle, a pickleball, a net, and a court. Comfortable sports clothing and shoes are also recommended.

Basic Rules

Pickleball can be played in singles or doubles. Players hit the ball over the net and try to score points by making the opponent miss.

Why Play Pickleball?

Pickleball is a fun way to stay active, improve coordination, and play with friends. It can be enjoyed by beginners and experienced players.

Getting Started

Start by learning how to hold the paddle, serve, and return the ball. Practice your movement and basic shots before playing a full game.
